Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who uses ShortsTool

ShortsTool is a personal desktop tool. Its only user is the owner of the accounts it posts to: YouTube channel Litvinchik, TikTok @litvinchik_ and Instagram @litvinchikl_. It uploads the owner's own gaming videos to the owner's own accounts.

Permissions we ask for

  • YouTube: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly — to upload the owner's videos and read their statistics and comments.
  • TikTok: user.info.basic — to show which TikTok account is connected; video.upload — to send the owner's shorts to the TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Instagram: instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish, instagram_business_manage_comments — to post the owner's reels and read their statistics and comments.

Where data is stored

Access tokens, videos and statistics stay only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform the video is posted to.

Sharing

No data is shared with or sold to third parties. No data of other users is collected.

Revoking access

  • YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites

After access is revoked, the tokens stored on the computer stop working and can be deleted.
