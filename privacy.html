Privacy policy

  • ShortsTool asks only for the permissions it needs to post the owner's videos and to read their basic statistics and comments: on YouTube youtube.upload and youtube.readonly; on TikTok user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload and video.list; on Instagram instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish and instagram_business_manage_comments.
  • Access tokens and all data stay only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform the video is posted to.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties. No data of other users is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (YouTube), TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (TikTok), Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites (Instagram).
