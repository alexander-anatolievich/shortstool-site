ShortsTool is a desktop app for a gaming creator. It takes the creator's own game recordings, finds the best moments, cuts them into vertical 9:16 shorts and sends them to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Features

Everything runs on the creator's own Windows PC. The creator records, ShortsTool does the editing work.

🎬

Episodes from recordings

Joins OBS recordings and the face camera into one finished 1440p episode with clean sound.

✂️

Shorts from the best moments

Finds funny and scary moments in the speech and sound and cuts each into a vertical 9:16 short.

🔇

Clean language

Speech is transcribed; swear words are muted on the voice track before anything is posted.

✅

Creator approves every short

Each short is shown to the creator first. Only shorts the creator accepted go out.

📅

Schedule

Accepted shorts are spread over the day so the channels get a steady flow of videos.

📊

Own statistics

Reads views and watch time of the creator's own videos to learn which shorts work better.

How it works

From a finished recording to a short on every platform.

  1. RecordThe creator plays and records with OBS, as usual.
  2. EditShortsTool builds the episode and the shorts on the PC: transcription, best moments, vertical crop, face camera, muted swearing.
  3. ReviewThe creator watches every short and accepts or rejects it.
  4. SendAccepted shorts go to the creator's own accounts through the official APIs: YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram API.

TikTok integration

ShortsTool connects to TikTok with Login Kit for Desktop and uses the Content Posting API in "Upload to inbox" mode. Nothing is posted automatically.

  1. ConnectThe creator signs in with TikTok and allows access (user.info.basic, video.upload). ShortsTool shows which TikTok account is connected.
  2. Upload as a draftA short the creator accepted is sent to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. Creator posts in TikTokThe creator opens the inbox notification in the TikTok app, edits the caption, chooses who can see it and posts it — or deletes the draft.

Only the creator's own original gameplay is uploaded. Access can be revoked any time in TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

FAQ

Who can use ShortsTool?

ShortsTool is a personal tool of the gaming creator Litvinchik. It posts only to the creator's own accounts: YouTube channel Litvinchik, TikTok @litvinchik_ and Instagram @litvinchikl_. There is no sign-up and no accounts of other users.

Where is my data stored?

Only on the creator's own computer. Videos and access tokens are not sent anywhere except to the platform the video is posted to. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it post to TikTok by itself?

No. It only puts a draft into the TikTok inbox. The creator finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.

What does it cost?

Nothing. ShortsTool is not sold and has no ads.
